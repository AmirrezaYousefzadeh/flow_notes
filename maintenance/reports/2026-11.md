## 7. Proposals and decisions for the owner

1. **Remedy A as a rule?** It saves 8 % (tt) and 19 % (1.28 V) energy on a logic-dominated core, but cost a
   utilisation step there (core +24 %). It doubles the hold delay cells on a small example. Options:
   - (a) make it a rule for all new sky130 work;
   - (b) a rule only for designs reported at low voltage;
   - (c) keep it optional and state it.

   My recommendation: (a), after the B-only run of the same core is measured, because the comparison that matters
   is now B against A+B.
2. **Hold margins.** The default margins fail hold at ff every time, and the notes already require hold at every
   corner. Options:
   - (a) keep the defaults and let the 9-corner sign-off reject and re-harden;
   - (b) a rule for a margin between 0.1 and 0.5 ns (for example 0.2 ns) that passes ff, measured on the reference set;
   - (c) `RUN_POST_GRT_RESIZER_TIMING: true` with its 0.05 ns margin.

   My recommendation: measure (b) and (c) on the adder-tree reference design, as a proposed heavy job of a few hours.
3. **Ladder behaviour on a crash:** it now stops (implemented, safe). The alternative is one automatic rerun of the
   same utilisation. Tell me if you prefer that.
4. **Pass to the projects** (I do not write into project folders):
   - KWS_neuromorphic_tech_node: its `harden_asap7.sh` (lines 162–180) and `harden_sky130.sh` (143–144) read OpenSTA's
     seconds as ps or ns. Every hold-retry margin is computed as 10 ps, which affects the live `tm1_b`/`tmh_b`
     decisions.
   - KWS_neuromorphic_tech_node: README/`env.sh` cite notes "sky130 5.1 / ASAP7 4.0"; its `amir_toughts.md` asks for
     the three `*FLOW_ISSUE*.md` files to be deleted once handled.

     `clkdlybuf4s*` exclusion (4 381 and 8 931 delay cells, 8–9 % of the cells) and keeps `CTS_APPLY_NDR: half`. It
     puts one SRAM22 tt liberty under `"*"` and signs off at tt only (a competition rule that, with default margins,
     accepts ff hold failures). `LEADERBOARD.md` still shows OpenLane 2 numbers. `data/raw/*.mat` are broken links to
     `/home/yousefzadeha/workspace/BCI_paper/data`.
   - Async_inference: `power/tools` was copied from a commit before the 2026-10-30 tool fixes (`vcd_toggles.c`,
